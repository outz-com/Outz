Privacy & safety

What we collect

When you place an order we only ask for your name, phone number and delivery address. We never ask for your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or OTP — and OUTZ CLOTHING staff will never call or message asking for these.

How payment works

Payment happens entirely inside your own UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) when you scan our QR code. We do not process, touch or store your bank or card details on this website at any point.

How your order is used

Your order details are used only to confirm, pack and ship your order, and to reach you if there's an issue with delivery. We don't sell or share your information with third parties.

Staying safe

All input on this site is filtered before it's stored or displayed, to stop malicious code or links from being submitted through the order form. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords with anyone claiming to be OUTZ — we will never ask.
